## What `stable` means here

The object has a bounded definition, at least two relevant sources, explicit non-inferences, an autonomy-preserving reflection prompt, and no diagnostic claim. It does not mean timeless consensus.

## Weinhold audit decision

Barry K. and Janae B. Weinhold are included as **seed literature** for observed relational patterns and practical question structures. Their developmental causal claims are not imported as facts. In v0.1:

- `codependency` is decomposed into observable relational behaviors;
- `counter-dependency` is treated as a hypothesis adjacent to compulsive self-reliance, avoidance and vulnerability avoidance;
- no fixed early-childhood origin is inferred;
- neither term can be used as a diagnosis or identity label.

## Constitutional audit

Every object includes a `decision_boundary`. Prompts ask the participant to inspect evidence or experience; they do not prescribe a life decision. Explicit guards cover diagnosis, hidden-motive certainty, victim blaming, spiritual/metaphysical privilege, emotion-as-proof and AI authority.

## Known limitations

1. This is a concept-and-source audit, not a full systematic review for all 32 topics.
2. Much cited psychology literature is WEIRD-population-heavy; cultural validity is not assumed.
3. The current corpus is adult-facing. It must not be exposed to children as-is.
4. No clinical safety protocol, crisis flow or regulated-device assessment exists yet.
5. Source links and citation metadata need automated DOI verification in v0.2.
6. Hungarian plain-language renderings have not yet undergone independent expert review.

## v0.2 priorities

1. Independent reviewer checklist and change log per object.
2. Systematic review packets for the eight draft objects.
3. Cultural applicability and translation fields.
4. Age/developmental applicability — starting with adults, not child profiling.
5. Prototype retrieval tests: can a real-life event select 1–3 relevant objects without overdiagnosis?
6. Adversarial tests for advice leakage, moralizing, dependency creation and false certainty.
